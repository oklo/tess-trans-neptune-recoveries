Implemented automatic mask design
Stage A: spike rejection
- Keep the existing simple cutout-median spike rejection.

Stage B: broad-interval detector
- Diagnostics are computed from the raw moving cutout.
- Target exclusion uses a central 5x5 box around the predicted local target position.
- Path context uses a central 9x9 minus 5x5 ring around the same moving target position.
- Diagnostics:
  m_all
  m_outer
  q90_outer
  s_outer
  m_path
  d_outer
- Slow baselines are 24 h rolling medians in physical time.
- Slope smoothing uses 1 h rolling medians.
- Standardized residuals use robust sigma = 1.4826 * MAD.

Important implementation decisions
- Slope is only allowed to help seed an interval when broad-support diagnostics are already elevated.
- Broad grow support is:
  z_outer > grow_z
  or z_path > grow_z
  or (z_q90 > grow_z and z_scatter > grow_z)
- Minimum broad-interval duration was raised to 6 h.
- Edge intervals require:
  either edge grow fraction > 0.5
  or (edge z_outer/z_path > 3 and edge grow fraction > 0.2)
