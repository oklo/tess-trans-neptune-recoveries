Interpretation
- The off-center feature is most consistent with a persistent fixed-source
  residual or blended source patch, not with an interpolation / edge-support
  artifact.
- The nearby faint TIC entries likely indicate a small source group or
  deblended components in the same neighborhood, while the Tmag 10.74 source
  suggests that bright-source wings or blend residuals are also plausible.
- The clipped nominal Pluto stack still behaves physically at the target
  location, but the strongest off-target supported peak is best interpreted as
  static-source contamination in a source-rich detector patch.

Implication for next smallest fix
- The next smallest fix is not a more elaborate global ramp cut.
- It is to add static-source masking or veto logic for persistent off-target
  supported peaks that map onto known catalog sources or source-rich patches.
- For Sedna-scale work, this suggests that background handling should include a
  catalog-aware contamination guard, not just broad cadence rejection and local
  background subtraction.
