Pluto Spillover-Masking Milestone
2026-04-09

Context
- This pass followed the raw-frame Pluto catalog-masked stack workflow on the
  full retained Sector 92 / camera 2 / CCD 1 bundle:
  outputs/pluto_full_duration_conservative_mask/pluto_sector92_cam2_ccd1_full_cube_bundle_masked.npz
- The earlier raw-frame nominal stack showed Pluto clearly at the center, but a
  brighter off-center peak remained at stack pixel (20, 15).

What changed
- The catalog-crosser selection was expanded so TIC/Gaia sources whose centers
  never enter the 21x21 cutout can still be included when their PSF wings spill
  into the cutout.
- This was implemented by widening the detector-footprint overlap rule in:
  scripts/list_pluto_catalog_crossers.py
- The raw-frame stack workflow in:
  scripts/run_pluto_catalog_mask_stack.py
  was then rerun using the expanded bright-source list.

Numerical change
- Bright masked sources increased from 63 to 88.
- Mean masked pixels per frame increased from about 187.3 to 191.2.
- Center aperture S/N stayed essentially unchanged:
  before = 57.53
  after  = 57.21
- The final stack peak moved from the contaminant at (20, 15) to the Pluto core
  neighborhood at (11, 10).

Interpretation
- Off-grid bright sources and their PSF wings were a real contamination mode.
- Expanding the catalog mask to include spillover sources removed the dominant
  outer-edge bright peak without materially weakening the central Pluto signal.
- This is a meaningful improvement over the earlier raw-frame Pluto stack and is
  a milestone for the Pluto calibration workflow.

Useful outputs

- outputs/pluto_full_duration_catalog_sources/pluto_sector92_cam2_ccd1_tic_crossers_bright.csv

Implication
- The next smallest background-handling fix for harder targets should include
  catalog-aware masking that covers both in-frame sources and off-grid spillover
  sources, not only stars whose centroids enter the cutout.
