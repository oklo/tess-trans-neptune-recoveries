Subset result
- The exact 32-frame bright subset from the stack audit was reused unchanged.
- Best subset x offset from the fine grid: -0.45 px
- Direct moving-aperture summary also favored -0.45 px on this subset.
- The subset stack score rose from about 18.53 at exact nominal to about 26.28 at x = -0.45 px.
- The nearby coarse x - 0.5 px point was almost identical at about 26.08, which is consistent with the earlier audit and suggests a real narrow preference near the negative half-pixel side.

Full frozen-bundle result
- Full-bundle best x offset from the same fine grid: -0.45 px
- The direct full-bundle metric was broader and favored about -0.25 px, but the stacked metric again preferred -0.45 px.
- The exact nominal full-bundle stack score was about 2.80.
- The calibrated x = -0.45 px full-bundle stack score improved to about 3.10.
- The subset and full stacked optima agree within 0.10 px, which argues that the negative x preference is not a fluke of only the bright subset.

Null-control comparison
- After centering the null-control gate on the calibrated x track, the calibrated track still did not beat nearby controls cleanly.
- Exact nominal score: about 2.80
- Calibrated-x score: about 3.10
- Best nearby / outer control score: about 4.08 at delta ( +0.5, +1.0 ) relative to the calibrated track
- Calibrated minus best control: about -0.98
- Interpretation: constant x calibration helps, but it is not sufficient to make the full Pluto track behave physically enough for a clean pass.

Cadence-growth comparison
- A nominal-versus-calibrated cadence ladder was run on the full frozen bundle.
- Final cumulative scores:
  nominal about 2.80
  calibrated about 3.10
- The calibrated curve was not uniformly better:
  at intermediate checkpoints (216, 432, 648 frames) the calibrated track was weaker than exact nominal
  only at the full 864-frame total did calibrated finish slightly above nominal
- Interpretation: the constant x correction is not producing a clean monotonic or consistently improved growth story. That argues against a pure fixed-convention bug being the whole explanation.

Time dependence / residual behavior
- Bright-subset centroid residuals remain consistent with the earlier audit:
  median residual x about -0.242 px
  median residual y about -0.012 px
- A sparse full-bundle centroid sample looks much less clean:
  median residual x about -0.057 px
  median residual y about +0.164 px
  residual scatter is large, with sigma_x about 0.72 px and sigma_y about 0.58 px
- The remaining mismatch is therefore better described as drifting or systematics-dominated rather than approximately constant.

Conclusion
- A constant local x calibration exists on this frozen Pluto bundle and is consistently near -0.45 px in the stacked metric.
- That correction improves the exact nominal Pluto track, but it does not make the calibrated full-bundle track beat nearby controls cleanly.
- Constant x calibration is therefore not enough to close the Pluto debugging loop.
- Pluto is not yet clean enough to justify returning to Sedna.
